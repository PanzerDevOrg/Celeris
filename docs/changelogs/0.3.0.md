# Changelog: Celeris v0.3.0 (1.21 – 26.3)

Celeris can now move items through pipes for mods that want visible item logistics: the pipes, the items travelling in them and what every player sees are all handled without one entity per item. Nothing existing changes: everything below is added to what 0.2.3 had.

---

## Key Features & Changes

* **Item Transport**: A new solver moves items along the pipe segments of a network, one tick at a time, with spacing between items, routers that decide where an item goes at a junction (round robin, priority and filter ones are included) and inventories at the ends of the pipes. The result is the same whatever order the pipes are stored in.
* **Items Survive Edits**: Placing or breaking pipes no longer loses the items inside. A mod that asks for it gets each item carried over to the pipe that now covers the same stretch, with the same speed and place; items on a stretch that no longer exists are handed back to the mod instead of vanishing.
* **Saving Networks**: The state of every segment (items in flight included) can be written and read back by position, so a saved world comes back with its items where they were. Files carry a version, and a malformed or too-new file is refused, never half applied.
* **Drawn Without Entities**: A client works out where each item is, every frame, from the last news about it. Servers send a small message only when something changes (an item enters, changes pipe, changes speed or leaves), not positions every tick. Messages are size-capped and checked as a whole before anything is applied.
* **Fast World Loads**: Adding many pipe blocks at once builds the whole network in one pass. 10,000 blocks in a straight line: 9 s → 3 ms; a 100 × 100 grid: 45 ms → 4 ms.
* **Watching a Network**: Mods can be told when pipe segments are created, removed or changed, and can look at the pipes around a block, without scanning the whole network every tick.
* **Fixes**: Changing the connections of a block in the middle of a pipe left an old segment behind. A pipe at position (-1, -1, -1) was mistaken for "no pipe" when a neighbour was removed. The continuous pipe solver copied its segment list and reallocated its scratch space every tick; it no longer does.
* **Checked Positions**: `NodeId.inRange` and `NodeId.packChecked` tell a mod when a position does not fit the packed form (the unchecked form wraps and would alias another block). Vanilla's world and height limits all fit.
* **Big Networks**: Pipes backed up behind a full consumer are no longer walked item by item every tick: 8 million items waiting behind refusing consumers cost 6 ms a tick instead of 29 ms. `TransitSolver.setWorkers` optionally shares the pass over flowing items with worker threads (the same result, bit for bit): 8 million flowing items, 42 ms → 18 ms with 3 workers.
* **Example Mod**: The pipe block of `celeris_example` now carries items: right-click a pipe end with an item, watch it travel, and see it go into a chest at the other end.

---

## Performance

* 100,000 items on 200-block pipes: 101 µs per tick against 261 µs for one object per item (2.6×). On 20-block pipes with a junction every 20 blocks it is a tie (326 µs against 317 µs): most of the time there is spent handing items over at junctions.
* The vector-based advance of item positions gives the same results as the plain loop and is not measurably faster; the speed-up above comes from the flat layout.
* See `docs/benchmarks.md` for the full tables.
